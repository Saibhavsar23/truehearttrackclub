01 — Who we are

Built by runners.
Not by a committee.

True Heart isn’t a track club in the traditional sense. It’s what you get when a group of high-school runners decides the sport they love deserves something that’s actually theirs.

It grew out of what we were already doing: racing, showing up to track meets and cross country courses, pushing each other, talking trash, cracking jokes the whole way through. At some point somebody pointed a camera at it. It kept going.

Now we meet runners, interview them, film the chaos and document the sport from the inside, through the eyes of the people actually racing it. Our community is centered around Watchung Hills High School and New Jersey high-school running, and that’s the world we show. We’re not an official school team or program. We’re runners with a camera and a lot to say.
